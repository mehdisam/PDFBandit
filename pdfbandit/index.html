A simple, careful process

How it works

Just like placing paper patches on a manual — but digital.

1

Find the text

Identify the text blocks you want to translate. Let PDFBandit detect them or draw the regions yourself.

2

Print your patches

Translate the text or recognize it with OCR, then render the result inside each patch.

3

Match the paper

Sample the page background to make your patches blend naturally into the original document.

4

Export your copy

Your translated document is ready. The original PDF remains untouched, and every patch can still be edited.
